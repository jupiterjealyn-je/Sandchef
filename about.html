How It All Started

SandChef is a sandwich business that offers delicious, freshly prepared, and affordable sandwiches for customers looking for a convenient and satisfying meal. What began as a simple student idea has grown into a small business serving fresh Empanabread every day.

Every order is made fresh — no shortcuts, no preservatives. Just good food at a price everyone can afford.

🎯

Our Mission

To serve fresh, filling meals that fit every budget, to use responsible packaging, to offer great promos for sharing, and to give fast, friendly service that makes every customer happy.

🌟

Our Vision

To be the top choice for affordable, tasty Empanabread and combo meals in Iligan City — known for fresh ingredients, student-friendly prices, and eco-friendly practices.
