About SandChef

A small student business with big flavor.

How It All Started

SandChef began as a simple idea: make fresh, affordable sandwiches that students can enjoy during recess. With a small budget and a big dream, we started preparing ham and chocolate-filled sandwiches right at home.

Every order is made fresh — no shortcuts, no preservatives. Just good food at a price everyone can afford.

🎯

Our Mission

To serve fresh, delicious, and affordable sandwiches to students, making every snack break a moment to enjoy.

🌟

Our Vision

To become the go-to snack brand in our school — known for quality, honesty, and a personal touch in every order.
