Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I place an order?

Go to our Order page, select your Empanabread, fill in your details, and click "Submit Order." Then send us a message via Messenger to confirm.

Do you deliver?

We currently offer pickup only at school. You can choose to meet at the main gate or arrange with the seller.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash (face-to-face) is our main method. GCash and Maya may also be available — please ask the seller.

How much does your Empanabread cost?

Empanabread starts at ₱15. Combo meals with juice are only ₱20.

Can I order in advance?

Yes! Just message us via Messenger and we'll prepare your order for a specific time — perfect for lunch break.

What are the ingredients?

Our Empanabread contains bread, ham or chocolate filling, and optional cheese or spreads. Please let us know if you have any allergies.

Do you have student discounts?

Our prices are already student-friendly! But watch out for seasonal promos like "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" during special weeks.

How long does an order take?

Empanabread is made fresh. Most orders are ready in 5–10 minutes.
